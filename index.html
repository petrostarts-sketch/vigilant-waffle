Questions

Before you order

Do you need my password?

No. Views, likes and followers are delivered using your public link — we never ask for account passwords or login access.

How fast does delivery start?

Most orders begin within 60 minutes and complete on a staggered schedule over hours or days, depending on the package, so growth looks gradual rather than instant.

How does a verification number work?

You'll get a number to sign in with. Once you've entered it on your end, we forward the OTP the moment it arrives so you can finish verifying. Every number carries a 7-day warranty — available for the United States and several other countries.

Is this against a platform's rules?

Purchased engagement and verification numbers can conflict with individual platforms' terms of service, and using them carries some risk to an account. Use your own judgment about what you're comfortable with.

What if an order doesn't complete?

Every order is tracked from your dashboard. If a package doesn't finish delivering, you can request a refill or refund for the missing portion.
